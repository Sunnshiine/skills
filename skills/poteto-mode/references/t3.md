# T3 Code

A thread launched with `t3_thread_launch` is a full chat. It runs a playbook, spawns its own subagents, and watches its own PRs. A `delegate_task` child is a leaf. T3 tells it to do the work itself, to spawn nothing its task doesn't name, and never to watch a PR.

## Spawning

A launched thread is a lasting chat in the user's sidebar, so launch one only for a **merge-bound owner**: the worker that opens a PR meant to merge and carries it to merged. Every other worker is a subagent.

- **Merge-bound owners** are launched threads, because only a thread can watch its PR. Use `workspaceStrategy: {type: "worktree", baseRef: "<trunk>"}`, or the parent branch with `startFromOrigin: false` for a stacked PR, and pass `delegatedFrom` with your own thread. A playbook that tells you to spawn a merge-bound owner is the user's request for that thread.
- **Everything else** goes to `delegate_task` with `mode: "async"`, plus `isolation: "worktree"` when it needs its own checkout: questions, investigations, prototypes, draft or throwaway PRs, leaf workers, and verifiers. Call `orchestrator_capabilities` once per thread before the first one. Give a verifier a Claude model other than its author's (see the `# Providers` line in the pstack settings file).
- **Quick helpers** whose result your next step needs use native `Agent` with `run_in_background: false`.
- **A coordinator that opens no PR**, such as an Autopilot-full root, runs in your own chat and launches its merge-bound owners itself.

## Kickoff

A launched thread's `message` opens with "Invoke the poteto-mode skill and run `playbooks/<name>.md`", or with "Invoke the `<name>` skill" when the work names a skill. Then it gives the restated goal, the evidence that proves done, the paths it may write, the actions it must not take, and every grant word for word. A launched thread inherits your runtime mode and can't exceed it, so run the coordinator in a mode that lets owners push and merge.

## Supervising

- **Wait** on the thread whose result unblocks your next step with `t3_thread_wait` and `untilDelegatedWorkSettled: true`. On a timeout that returns `next`, end your turn. For a delegate, follow its `next`. The hourly `/loop` covers every other worker.
- **Probe** with `t3_thread_read`, which leaves an idle thread idle.
- **Follow up on the same work** with `t3_thread_send`. A new round goes to a fresh thread.
- **Stop** a launched thread with `t3_thread_interrupt` and a delegate with `task_cancel`.
- **Name** each thread by its `link` in `notes.md` and in replies.

## `/loop`

`schedule_task` with `schedule: {type: "interval", everyMs: 3600000}` and `bindToCurrentThread: true`. Pause it with `enabled: false` once no delegated work is left.

## PRs

The owner calls `link_pull_request` right after opening its PR. In T3, `watch_pull_request` replaces babysit's `watch-pr` drive under `/loop`. While the PR waits on checks or reviews, the owner watches it and ends its turn, and T3 wakes it on the next event. Babysit's `check` mode (`watch-pr --status-only`) still reads the state. The owner calls `unwatch_pull_request` before handback.
